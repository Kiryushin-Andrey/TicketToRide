# Pip the Little Train Who Wanted to Say "Choo-Choo!"

Once upon a time, in a land of marshmallow hills and lemonade rivers, there lived a tiny red train named Pip.

Pip was the smallest train in the whole wide world. All the big trains went *CHOO-CHOO!* But when Pip tried, only a little squeaky sound came out: *"pffft-tweet!"*

"Hee hee!" giggled the sheep. "You sound like a squeaky toy!"

Pip felt sad. So he set off to find his very own *choo-choo*.

First, Pip met a **cow**. "Moo can help!" said the cow. "Say *MOOOO!*"
Pip tried: *"Mooo-pffft!"* The cow laughed so hard she fell into a puddle of milk.

Next, Pip met a **frog**. "Ribbit! Try jumping!"
Pip jumped. *Boing!* He landed in a tree and got stuck with a bird on his head.

Then Pip met a **sleepy bear**. "Shhh," yawned the bear. "Say it softly, from your tummy."

Pip took a deep breath. He thought about his friends: the cow, the frog, the bird, and the bear. His tummy felt warm and happy.

And out came...

### *"CHOO-CHOO-TWEET-MOO-RIBBIT!"* 🚂

It was the loudest, silliest, most wonderful sound ever! All the animals cheered and hopped on board for a ride across the marshmallow hills.

From that day on, Pip was never sad again. He knew a secret: you don't need to sound like everybody else. You just need to sound like **you**.

**The End** 🌈
