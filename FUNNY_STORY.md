# The Great Train Mix-Up

Gerald had one goal: travel from Seattle to Miami by rail, completing his Destination Ticket before his rival, Brenda, finished hers.

He had 45 little plastic trains, a deck of colorful cards, and a dream.

"I'll just need six blue cards," he told himself, drawing a card. It was a Locomotive. He gasped. Wild cards are rare! He drew another. Also a Locomotive. Gerald began to suspect the universe liked him.

The universe did not.

On his next turn, Gerald proudly claimed the long route from Seattle to Helena, only to discover he had actually claimed the route to **Hellena**, a tiny town that, according to the box, did not exist.

"That's not a real place," said Brenda.

"It is now," said Gerald, who had already placed his trains.

By turn twelve, Gerald's network looked less like a railway and more like a plate of spaghetti someone had dropped from a great height. One track looped around Chicago three times. Another led directly into a lake.

"That's a very *scenic* route," said Brenda, placing her final train in Miami with a satisfying click.

"I'm not losing," Gerald whispered. "I'm *sightseeing*."

He scored negative 21 points for his unfinished ticket. The cat, who had been sitting on the board the entire game, scored zero and was declared the most efficient player at the table.

**THE END**

*No trains were harmed in the making of this story. Several were lost in Hellena.*
