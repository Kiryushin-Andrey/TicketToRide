# The Great Ticket Mix-Up

A conductor named Sam prided himself on never losing a single ticket stub in twenty years on the job. One foggy morning, he boarded the 7:15 express with his usual thermos of coffee and a brand-new ticket punch, fresh out of the box.

Somewhere around the third stop, Sam reached into his pocket for his punch and pulled out... a bagel. He checked his other pocket: his punch, miraculously, had been swapped with a sesame bagel from the station cafe.

Determined not to break his streak, Sam improvised. He "punched" each ticket by taking a small bite out of the corner. Passengers stared, bemused, as their tickets came back with a neat little crescent missing and a faint smell of toasted sesame.

By the last stop, Sam had eaten his way through half a box of tickets disguised as punch marks, gained three new regular passengers who found the whole thing hilarious, and discovered his actual punch sitting snugly in the cafe's bagel basket, right where he'd set down his breakfast.

The cafe owner later admitted she'd nearly stapled a bagel to a receipt before noticing it wouldn't toast properly.

Sam kept the bitten tickets as souvenirs. To this day, nobody knows how the swap actually happened — the leading theory on the train is that the bagel simply wanted to see the world.
