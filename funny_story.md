# The Great Train Delay

Barnaby the conductor had one rule: *the train must never be late.*

One Tuesday, he discovered his pocket watch was exactly seven minutes fast. Rather than fix it, he reasoned, "If I simply leave seven minutes early, I'll always be on time!"

Then the passengers learned the trick and began arriving seven minutes early too. So Barnaby left even earlier. So did the passengers. Within a week, everyone was arriving at the station the previous day.

By Friday, the 9:00 train departed Thursday at 9:00, carrying one very confused goat who had simply wandered aboard to nap.

The goat was the only passenger ever to arrive exactly on time. The railway promoted it to Head of Scheduling.

Barnaby fixed his watch. Nobody noticed, and the goat still gets the better parking spot.

**The End.**
