Water temperature guide for swimming:

- Below 15°C (59°F) — cold, only for experienced cold-water/open-water swimmers, risk of cold shock
- 16–20°C (61–68°F) — cool, tolerable for short swims if acclimatized
- 21–24°C (70–75°F) — refreshing, fine for most active swimmers
- 25–29°C (77–84°F) — the sweet spot most people consider "warm enough" for comfortable swimming
- 30°C+ (86°F+) — warm to hot; pleasant for lounging but can feel too warm for vigorous exercise

Competitive pools: ~25–28°C
Leisure/therapy pools: ~29–32°C
