# The Last Train to Millbrook

Every Sunday for forty years, Edmund and Rosa played their favorite route-building board game at the kitchen table. Edmund always chose the blue trains. Rosa always chose the red.

Rosa had a habit of quietly leaving the longest route unclaimed until the very end, then snatching it with a smile. "Patience wins the rails," she'd say.

When Rosa passed away in the spring, Edmund kept the table set. Each Sunday he laid out the board, placed the red trains in their little pile across from him, and played both sides. He always let the red trains win.

One Sunday, the longest route, the one from Millbrook to the sea, was still open. Edmund held his blue trains over it for a long while. Then he set them down and left it empty.

"Your turn," he whispered to the empty chair.

The route is still unclaimed. He's waiting for her.
