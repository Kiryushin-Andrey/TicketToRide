A rubber duck walks into a code review. The senior engineer says, "Wait, you're
not allowed in here, you're just for debugging." The duck replies, "I know, I
just wanted to see what happens when nobody explains anything to me out loud."

Turns out the PR had an off-by-one error that three humans missed — but the
duck caught it, because it had been patiently listening to someone talk
through the loop logic at 2am the night before, and that explanation never
got written into the code's comments, only muttered aloud.

The moral, taped above the senior engineer's desk the next morning:
"If you can't explain it to the duck, you can't explain it to the next person
reading this either."
