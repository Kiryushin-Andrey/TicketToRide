The Bug That Wasn't

A junior developer spent three days tracking down a bug where the production server
crashed every day at exactly 4:17 PM. He checked cron jobs, memory leaks, network
timeouts, even solar flare data. Nothing matched.

On day four, he finally found it: the cleaning lady unplugged the server every
afternoon to plug in her vacuum cleaner, then plugged it back in a few minutes later.
The "crash" was just the server rebooting. The "fix" was a sticky note that said
"DO NOT UNPLUG - EXPENSIVE COMPUTER" taped next to the outlet.

The post-mortem report listed the root cause as "insufficient PDU redundancy."
Everyone just called it "the vacuum incident."
